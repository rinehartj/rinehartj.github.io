The inner diameter of the brush attachment and outer diameter of the long tube were measured using a digital caliper. The dimensions only needed have a precision of 0.5mm because the attachment and tube were both chamfered at the factory. This part was modeled mostly by my dad and manufactured by me. We tried out SolidCraft open-source 3D-modeling and found that it is suitable for small jobs like this, and possibly more complicated models in the future.
