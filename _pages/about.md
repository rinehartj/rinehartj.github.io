Hi there! My name is Jason Rinehart, and as a fun fact, I have ridden over 3,000 miles by electric scooter...yes, while wearing a helmet. I maintain this website as a living showcase of my engineering projects and accomplishments. In my free time, I work on 3D printing, breadboard prototyping, self-hosting and network management. I use these skills to help myself and the people around me. As an example, I built a working computer out of e-waste for my parents.

I am a believer that hard work leads to a life of many accomplishments. As such, I try to always keep my brain occupied with new challenges that are slightly more difficult than the last. This way, I develop deep knowledge of the subjects that always puzzled me when I was younger, like how computers work and how to cook tasty food.
